Ministry Leadership

1. Servant leadership and character
Healthy ministry leadership begins with integrity, humility and consistent service.
Reflection: How can you apply this principle?

2. Vision, planning and team roles
Translate a clear ministry vision into measurable priorities. Assign responsibilities, timelines and review points.
Reflection: How can you apply this principle?

3. Communication and accountability
Listen actively, give respectful feedback, and resolve disagreements with fairness. Review progress with the whole team.
Reflection: How can you apply this principle?

Learning activity
Prepare a one-page plan for a ministry team, including roles, goals and a review date.
