Foundations of Theology — Class presentation

SLIDE 1

The nature and purpose of theology

Theology is disciplined reflection on God, Scripture and the life of faith.

SLIDE 2

Scripture, faith and Christian doctrine

Read a passage in context before drawing a doctrinal conclusion. Distinguish observation, interpretation and application.

SLIDE 3

Connecting belief with ministry practice

Discuss how beliefs shape worship, service and daily decisions. Use biblical references to support each argument.
