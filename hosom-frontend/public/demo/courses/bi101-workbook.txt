Biblical Interpretation

1. Literary and historical context
Interpret a passage in its literary context and consider its original audience, genre and purpose.
Reflection: How can you apply this principle?

2. Observation, interpretation and application
Observation asks what the text says. Interpretation asks what it means. Application asks how its teaching shapes conduct.
Reflection: How can you apply this principle?

3. Responsible use of cross references
Use cross references to clarify themes while respecting the meaning of each passage. Avoid building a conclusion on an isolated phrase.
Reflection: How can you apply this principle?

Learning activity
Read Luke 10:25-37. Record observations, explain the setting, and propose one practical application.
